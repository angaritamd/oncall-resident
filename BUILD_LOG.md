# Build log

## 2026-10-26 — Day 1 (after kickoff) · ANG-170

**Works for real (no LLM, no Muse Code, no Meta API):**
- Orthanc (DICOMweb) seeded with 5 public LIDC-IDRI chest studies (2 radiographs, 3 CT; CC BY 3.0) plus synthetic order metadata.
- `core` (FastAPI): rule-based intents over QIDO-RS:
  - "¿Qué tengo pendiente?" returns a summary, the next study and the full list.
  - "¿Por qué va primero?" explains where the priority comes from (technologist flag or order, never the model).
  - "Abrir DEMO-000N" opens the case. Priors are shown two ways: what the order declares, and what is actually in this PACS.
- `app` (Next.js PWA, dark, ES/EN): chat, Pendientes and Actividad tabs, a case view with back to chat, and loading, empty, offline and PACS-failure states with retry.
- Demo stack on the hack server, as a separate compose project, reachable over Tailscale only.
- Scanning: gitleaks, an external denylist, PHI patterns and DICOM detection, run by git hooks and CI.
- Tests: 13 core unit tests and 10 Playwright e2e runs in Brave (390 px mobile and desktop).

**Not available yet (shown as unavailable in the UI, never simulated):** LLM, microphone/voice, image viewer, the "necesita confirmación" state, authentication, the RadMesh adapter (stub only), serving/, radqc/.

**Known limits:** arrival times are offsets from core start, and the activity log is in memory. Both reset when the core restarts.

## 2026-10-27 — Day 2 · ANG-170 (UI aprobada + PWA + HTTPS)

- From now on: PRs into `main` with CodeRabbit review (`.coderabbit.yaml`: Spanish reviews, focus on security, error handling and no simulated features).
- The light UI follows the approved spec (the tokens, Inter and JetBrains Mono, ink/surface composition, a single blue accent, lucide icons):
  - **01 Inicio de turno**: greeting with the live queue count, the next study (ink), Críticos/Urgentes counts from the worklist, and quick prompts. "Ver pendientes" works; the other two are marked *Disponible pronto*.
  - **02 Pendientes**: filter pills with counts, "Críticos · tecnólogo" rows (ink), and other studies by arrival order (oldest first) in a white card.
  - **03 Caso abierto**: the case card is in the chat (ink). It shows synthetic sex and age, the shifted DICOM date, the indication, the technologist note (crit-dk when flagged) and priors (declared vs. found in PACS, "Sin dato" when unknown). "Ver imágenes" and "Ver previo" are marked *Disponible pronto*. Suggested replies follow the card.
  - 04/05 (draft, RAD·QC, shift close) are not built, and there is no sign button: signing is human only.
- Installable PWA:
  - Manifest "Residente", standalone, theme `#E9ECF0`.
  - R monogram icons at 192 and 512, plus maskable and Apple touch icons.
  - iOS splash screens for 4 iPhone sizes and safe areas. Generated by `app/scripts/gen-icons.mjs`.
- HTTPS: on hack the app binds to `127.0.0.1:13000` and is published with `tailscale serve` on 443 (tailnet only, no Funnel). The Let's Encrypt certificate is valid.
- Tests: 13 core tests. 18 Playwright + Brave e2e runs (390 px and desktop) over the https URL, which check the flows, ≥ 44 px touch targets, no horizontal scroll, the composer not covering content, error/offline/retry states, and the manifest and icons.
- Deviation from the spec: inactive tabs use `muted` instead of `muted-d`. `#9AA3AE` on `#E9ECF0` has 2.2:1 contrast, below WCAG AA.
